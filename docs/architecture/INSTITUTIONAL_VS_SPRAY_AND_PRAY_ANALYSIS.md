### Spray-and-Pray Approach:
- **Position Sizing**: 50% risk per trade (astronomical)
- **Leverage**: 20x (extreme gambling)
- **Stop Loss**: Wide and often ignored
- **Trade Frequency**: 46 signals/day = taking 95% of all signals
- **Win Rate**: 26% (3 losses for every 1 win)
- **Philosophy**: "Volume over quality - take every possible trade"

### Institutional Grade Approach:
- **Position Sizing**: 0.74-1.59% risk per trade (conservative)
- **Leverage**: None (cash trading)
- **Stop Loss**: Tight and respected (immediate exits)
- **Trade Frequency**: 2 signals/day = rejecting 98% of signals
- **Win Rate**: 100% (perfect execution of high-quality setups)
- **Philosophy**: "Quality over quantity - patience for perfect setups"

   - Volatility: Within acceptable trading range

### Quality Control:
- **85%+ Confluence**: Trade execution approved
- **70-84% Confluence**: Signal rejected (too risky)
- **<70% Confluence**: Signal rejected (insufficient setup)
